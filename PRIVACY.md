# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-04_

Auto-Like for AniList does not collect, track or sell personal data. It has no analytics and no server of its own.

## What is stored (locally, in your browser only)

- Your AniList **Client ID** and the **access token** AniList issues when you connect (valid for about one year)
- Your AniList **username** (to display "Connected as…")
- Your settings: speed, delays, limit per run, request cap, home feed, blacklist, interface language

The extension never asks for or stores your Client Secret.

## What is sent, and where

- To **AniList** (`anilist.co` for the login page, `graphql.anilist.co` for the API): the access token and the requests needed to read activities and like them, as the AniList website does for you.
- Nowhere else. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

## What the extension reads

- Pages on `anilist.co`, only to add its button and to know whether you are on your home feed or a profile.
- The address of your active tab, only when you open the popup, to tell you whether you should open or refresh an AniList page. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

## Your control

- **Disconnect account** in the popup deletes the stored token.
- Removing the extension deletes all stored data.
- You can revoke the token at any time in your AniList settings under **Apps**, or delete your API client on the AniList developer page.
